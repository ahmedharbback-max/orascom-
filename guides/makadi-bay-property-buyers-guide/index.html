Buying property in Makadi Bay: a practical buyer checklist

Researching a home on Egypt’s Red Sea coast? Compare the full cost and the documents behind an offer—not just the headline price or monthly instalment.

Editorial guide · Last reviewed: 5 November 2026 · General information, not legal or financial advice

Makadi Bay real estate attracts buyers comparing coastal homes near Hurghada. Makadi Bay is part of Egypt’s Red Sea coast, near Hurghada. Makadi Heights is a destination developed by Orascom Development. The developer describes it as a year-round community, elevated 78 metres above sea level, with its first full-time residents welcomed in 2022. See the official Orascom Development project profile for current project-level information.

1. Identify the exact home—not just the project

For searches such as chalets for sale in Makadi Bay, confirm whether the advertised property is legally classified as an apartment, chalet, townhouse or villa, and check the exact unit reference and title documents.

Ask for the phase or neighbourhood, unit reference, building or plot number, floor, orientation, view, internal and external areas, bedroom count, finishing specification and a floor plan. Confirm whether the quoted area is built-up, net internal or includes terraces and gardens. Ask which items shown in marketing images are included in the sale.

2. Get the complete price in writing

A starting price is not a complete purchase cost. Request a dated quotation for the exact unit, showing the total price, currency, reservation amount, down payment, instalment dates and amounts, delivery-linked payments, maintenance or service charges, club or facility fees, taxes and any other payable costs. Ask how long the quotation remains valid.

Unit specification

Exact unit ID, phase, layout, area calculation, orientation and finishing schedule.

Payment schedule

Every instalment, due date, late-payment terms and any delivery-linked amount.

Additional costs

Maintenance, service, utility connection, club and administrative charges, where applicable.

Delivery terms

Contractual delivery date, grace period, handover standard and remedies for delay.

3. Understand the payment plan

Compare the total amount payable over the full term—not only the down payment or monthly figure. Check whether instalments are equal, front-loaded, linked to construction milestones or due on handover. Ask whether the price changes with the selected plan and whether any amount is denominated in a currency other than EGP.

4. Review the contract and reservation conditions

Before transferring money, independently verify the identity and authority of the seller and the account receiving payment. Read the reservation form and sale contract carefully. Check cancellation and refund conditions, assignment or resale restrictions, late-payment consequences, force-majeure clauses, handover conditions and the process for registering or documenting your rights. Have an independent Egyptian real-estate lawyer review the documents; do not rely only on a salesperson’s explanation.

5. Verify delivery and ongoing costs

Ask for the contractual delivery date for your specific unit, what “finished” means, what is included at handover, and how snagging or defects are handled. Request the maintenance calculation, payment frequency, what it covers and whether it can change. Ask which facilities are operational now and which are planned; do not assume every advertised amenity is already open or included.

6. Treat rental and investment claims carefully

Do not treat projected rental income, occupancy, resale value or capital appreciation as guaranteed. If you are buying as an investment, build conservative scenarios that include furnishing, management, maintenance, vacancy, taxes, transaction costs and periods with no rental income. Seek independent comparable evidence and professional tax or legal advice where needed.

7. Visit and verify the location

Use the Makadi Heights location guide and map to plan a visit. Orascom Development states that the destination is around 15 minutes from Hurghada International Airport and 20 minutes from Hurghada Downtown; actual journey times depend on route and traffic. Visit the exact phase, inspect access roads and surroundings, and ask to see the facilities relevant to your decision.

Prices, availability, payment plans, delivery dates and facility status can change. Confirm every material detail in current written documents before making a payment.

Questions worth asking the sales team

  • What is the exact unit code and which phase is it in?
  • Can I receive the floor plan, area breakdown and finishing specification?
  • What is the total amount payable, including all fees?
  • What are the exact instalment dates and late-payment terms?
  • What is the contractual handover date and what happens if it is delayed?
  • Which facilities are open today, and which are still planned?
  • Can my independent lawyer review the reservation and sale documents before payment?

Frequently asked questions

Are online property prices final?

Not necessarily. Availability, unit specifications, phase and payment terms can change. Ask for a dated written quotation identifying the exact unit and every payable amount.

What should I check before paying a reservation deposit?

Verify the seller and recipient, exact unit reference, reservation and refund terms, total price, payment schedule, additional fees and the documents you will receive. Have the contract reviewed by an independent Egyptian property lawyer.

Can I assume rental income or capital appreciation?

No. Rental demand, occupancy, operating costs, resale liquidity and future prices are uncertain. Treat projections as scenarios, not guarantees, and verify them independently.

Where can I verify information about Makadi Heights?

Use the official Orascom Development destination page for project-level facts, and request current unit-specific information and documents directly from an authorised sales representative.
