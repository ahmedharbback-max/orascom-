Makadi Heights as an investment: what buyers should check

Makadi Heights is a coastal residential destination in Makadi Bay near Hurghada. Assess the exact apartment, townhouse or villa, total ownership costs and realistic exit options—not only the headline price or a projected rental return.

This is independent educational content, not the official Orascom Development website and not a recommendation to buy or a guarantee of investment returns.

1. Define the investment objective

Decide whether the property is for personal use, occasional holidays, long-term letting or short-term holiday rental. Each strategy has different costs and risks; a home suited to personal use may not produce the strongest rental economics.

2. Evaluate the exact unit

Compare its location, view, floor, layout, usable area, condition, privacy and access. Request the current floor plan, specifications, ownership and contract documents. For resale, independently verify the seller’s ownership and outstanding obligations.

3. Build a conservative rental model

Do not treat advertised rent, occupancy or annual yield as guaranteed. Include realistic occupied nights, platform commissions, cleaning, utilities, furnishing, maintenance, management, taxes and periods without tenants. Confirm whether the intended letting model is permitted.

4. Calculate total ownership costs

Include the purchase price, reservation and contract payments, maintenance, utilities, furnishing, insurance where relevant, taxes, transaction costs and resale or assignment fees. Request a written breakdown for the exact unit.

5. Check resale, delivery and facilities

Ask about assignment restrictions, approvals, transfer fees and outstanding instalments. For off-plan homes, verify contractual delivery and remedies for delay. Distinguish operating facilities from planned ones, and confirm beach access and service terms in writing.

6. Get independent advice

Have an independent Egyptian property lawyer review the reservation form, sale contract, payment schedule, ownership documents, cancellation, refund, late-delivery and resale clauses. Never rely only on verbal assurances or projected returns.

9. Compare the full price list and payment terms

When reviewing Makadi Heights prices 2026, a price list or starting prices, check the date, currency, unit reference and whether the figure is still valid. The answer depends on purchase price, unit quality, rental permissions, achievable rent, occupancy, running costs, management, resale liquidity and your holding period. Makadi Heights rental investment should be assessed using conservative scenarios rather than promised returns. Frequently asked questions

Is Makadi Heights a guaranteed rental investment?

No. Rental demand, occupancy, achievable rent, operating costs and resale values can change. No rental return or capital appreciation is guaranteed.

What costs should I include beyond the purchase price?

Review maintenance, utilities, furnishing, management, cleaning, taxes, transaction charges and any resale or assignment fees. Ask for a written unit-specific breakdown.

Can I buy for resale or rental?

Availability and letting or resale rules depend on the exact unit, phase, contract and current terms. Verify them before committing.
