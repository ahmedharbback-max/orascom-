Makadi Bay, El Gouna or Sahl Hasheesh? A practical buyer comparison

Makadi Bay, El Gouna, Sahl Hasheesh and Hurghada offer different settings and day-to-day considerations. The right choice depends on how you plan to use the property, your budget, access needs and the exact unit available.

This is independent educational content, not the official Orascom Development website and not a recommendation to buy or a guarantee of investment returns.

1. Makadi Bay real estate: Makadi Heights

Makadi Bay is south of Hurghada. Orascom Development describes Makadi Heights as a year-round coastal community with an elevated setting reaching 78 metres above sea level. Its official profile highlights sports facilities, a medical centre, clubhouse, children’s facilities, preschool and entertainment hub. Confirm which facilities currently operate and what access is included with a specific unit.

2. El Gouna

El Gouna is an established Red Sea town north of Hurghada, known for its marina and lagoon-oriented layout, hospitality offering and varied residential districts. Compare the exact neighbourhood, property type, service charges, access arrangements and current resale or primary-market availability.

3. Sahl Hasheesh

Sahl Hasheesh is a resort-oriented coastal destination south of Hurghada. Review the specific development, beach and hotel access terms, year-round services, transport, maintenance charges and whether the property suits full-time living or mainly holiday use.

4. Hurghada city

Hurghada offers a more urban environment with established neighbourhoods, shops, services and transport. The experience varies by district, building quality, proximity to the sea and management of the individual property.

5. Compare real ownership factors

Compare the exact purchase price and fees, unit size and condition, delivery and handover, maintenance and utilities, beach and facility access, transport and healthcare, year-round services, rental permissions and resale conditions. Ask each seller for the same dated information.

6. Compare prices fairly

Online asking prices may refer to different phases, sizes, finishing, views, delivery dates or payment schedules. Compare total cash price and total payable amount for equivalent units; do not assume an advertised starting price means current availability.

Important: Prices, availability, fees, access rules and delivery terms can change. Check the current unit documents, quotation and contract before paying.

Frequently asked questions

Is Makadi Heights better than El Gouna?

There is no universal winner. Compare the exact neighbourhood, unit, total ownership cost, services, access rules and intended use.

Is Makadi Bay cheaper than Sahl Hasheesh?

Prices vary by project, size, condition, view, delivery status and payment terms. Use current written quotations for comparable units.

What should I compare before buying on the Red Sea?

Compare unit specifications, total price and fees, payment schedule, delivery, maintenance, facility and beach access, transport, rental rules and resale conditions.
