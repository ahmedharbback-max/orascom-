PROJECT INFORMATION

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about the location, homes, everyday life and how to request current project details.

Where is Makadi Heights located?

Makadi Heights is a coastal residential community in Makadi Bay, near Hurghada, in Egypt’s Red Sea Governorate.

Who is the developer?

Makadi Heights is a project by Orascom Development. This website is an independent information and enquiry website, not the developer’s official website.

What types of homes are available?

The community includes apartments, townhouses, twinhouses and villas. Specific layouts, prices and availability can change; request the latest details before making a decision.

Can residents live there throughout the year?

Makadi Heights has an established residential community and a range of operating everyday services and facilities. Service availability and operating hours may change, so confirm current details before visiting.

How can I check prices and payment plans?

Prices, payment plans, delivery schedules and unit availability may change. Contact the sales team through the enquiry form or WhatsApp to request a current written offer.

How does beach access work?

Access to beaches or hotel facilities may depend on the specific unit, agreement, eligibility rules and current terms. Confirm in writing exactly what is included before purchase; do not assume access is included.

What defines the destination’s setting?

Orascom Development describes Makadi Heights as a year-round coastal community. Its official profile highlights an elevated setting reaching 78 metres above sea level.

What facilities are operating?

Operating amenities include Metropolitan International American School, fitness and club facilities, tennis, football and padel courts, We Care Clinics with an emergency room, retail and daily services, restaurants and cafés, and transport connections including Go Bus.

Are prices and availability guaranteed?

No. Unit sizes and price ranges are indicative and may change. Contact the sales team for current availability, final pricing and payment terms.

Is there an operating hospital?

A hospital is planned for the future. It is not currently an operating facility. We Care Clinics and an emergency room are listed among current healthcare services.
