THE DESTINATION

A coastal town
made for every season.

Makadi Heights is an Orascom Development destination in Makadi Bay, south of Hurghada. Its masterplan spans approximately 1,200 feddan, with natural elevations reaching up to 79 metres and a low 12% built-up footprint.

The coastal extension has enabled a beach experience of its own. Homeowners can enjoy access to the sea through the private beach or via the destination’s hotels, subject to the applicable access arrangements.
