Payment plans

Payment plans

Payment structures may vary by release, unit, buyer eligibility and date. No down payment percentage or instalment duration is stated here as a current offer because a current dated official schedule has not been independently verified. Request the complete schedule in writing, including any booking deposit, instalment dates, interest or financing charges, and late-payment terms.

Other ownership costs

Confirm the current maintenance deposit or annual fee, club or facility charges, parking, utility connection costs, taxes, registration expenses and any resale or assignment fees. Ask which amounts are one-off and which recur, and whether they are calculated by unit area or another basis.

Delivery timing is unit- and contract-specific. Use the delivery date stated in the signed contract and check the handover conditions rather than relying on a general estimate.
